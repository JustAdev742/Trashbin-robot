Get started

  1. Flash the wearable. Import sunburn-device.mkcd at makecode.microbit.org, download it to a micro:bit V2 and switch it on: it shows a tick.
  2. Connect. Tap Connect Bluetooth above and choose "BBC micro:bit". On Android, Chrome may need Location switched on to find it. No micro:bit yet? Try a demo device works without one.
  3. Share your location. Tap Use my location so the live UV index for where you are reaches the wearable every 10 minutes.

    Sunscreen guide
    • How much: one teaspoon (5 mL) for each arm, each leg, the front of the body, the back, and the face, neck and ears. That is 7 teaspoons, about 35 mL, for a full-body application.
    • Which: SPF 50+, broad spectrum, water resistant. Put it on 20 minutes before going out.
    • When again: every 2 hours (the device's timer), and straight after swimming, sweating or towelling off.
    • Not only sunscreen: a hat, sunglasses, covering clothing and shade. Sunscreen alone is not enough when the UV index is 8 or more.
    • UV bands: 0–2 low, 3–5 moderate, 6–7 high, 8–10 very high, 11+ extreme. Protection is needed from UV 3 upwards, even on cool or cloudy days.

      Tools

      Appearance

      Calibrate with the sensor in full sun, on the batteries you will wear, once the live UV above is fresh. The corrected scale lives in the device's memory until it restarts; copy the numbers into applySensorPreset to keep them.

      The USB speed must match the wearable's usbSpeed setting (1 = 115200, 2 = 9600). The volume goes to the wearable as vol= and lasts until it restarts; the lasting setting is soundVolume.

      How it works
      
    
      The micro:bit has no internet, so this page does the online half of the flowchart: it finds your location, asks Open-Meteo for the UV index there right now, and sends uv=… to the wearable over Bluetooth (or USB). The device uses whichever is higher, its own sensor or that value, and sends its status back every two seconds. Everything you see here is stored only in this browser.
      
    
      Browser support
      
    
      Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS and ChromeOS. On iPhone and iPad use the Bluefy browser. USB: Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the micro:bit plugged in. The device must be running Sunburn firmware 3.0 or later with "No Pairing Required".
      
    
      UV bands and colours follow the WHO Global Solar UV Index. Sunscreen amounts follow Cancer Council Australia.
